---
title: "Owners and teams"
description: "A Loupe project has one owner. Loupe has no teams yet."
---

Each Loupe project belongs to one account, its owner. The owner is the account
that created the project.

Loupe has no teams yet. A project has no second member, and you cannot share it
with a group. Some pages are for the owner alone, such as the
[Agent readiness tab and the Repositories section](../using/projects.md#agent-readiness)
of the project settings.

Several agents can work for one owner. A coding agent signs in through the
owner's account. See [First steps](index.md) and [Installing the CLI](cli.md).
