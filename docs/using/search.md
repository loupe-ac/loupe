---
title: Search
description: "Find pages, cards, and documents in one project or all owned projects from the top bar."
---

Every page has a search field in the top bar, left of **New work**. Press Cmd+K on macOS or Ctrl+K elsewhere to put the cursor in the field.

Type to search. The results appear under the field after a short pause in typing. They show up to 10 rows in three groups: Cards, Documents, and Pages. The groups take turns, so a group with many matches does not hide the others.
A card row shows the card number, its title, and its column. A document row shows its title and its status.

On project pages, card and document results belong to the current project. On Account and All projects, the search covers every project you own, and each result names its project. Projects owned by another account never appear.

Press Enter, or select **All results for …**, to open the full results page with every match.
Press Escape, or click outside the field, to close the results. The field keeps your text. Escape also moves the focus out of the field.

Select a card result to open it. On the board, the card opens in the side panel and the board stays in place. On every other page, the card page opens.
Select a document or page result to open it.

If a search fails, the results show **Could not search**. Select **Try again** to repeat it.
Before the first answer, the results show a **Searching…** line. During a later search, the old results stay on screen and a progress line shows on the field. A slow answer to an older query never replaces the results of the text now in the field.

## Narrow pages and busy pages

Below the large breakpoint, the field shows as a search icon. Select the icon to open the field over the top bar. Select **Cancel** or press Escape to close it.
A page that puts its own actions in the top bar, such as a document in review, also shows the icon at any width.

Without JavaScript, the field on a wide screen still works as a form. Type a query and press Enter to open the full results page.

## What a query matches

A card or document search matches the typed text anywhere inside a word, in the title or the text, and ignores case. For example, `pag` finds "Paging" and "Homepage".
Quotes, a leading `-` and `or` work as in `card_search`. For example, `"board search" -backlog` finds cards with the phrase and without the word "backlog".
Enter a card number, such as `#12`, to find that card first.
A query with no letters or digits, such as `!!!`, matches nothing.
Results include completed cards and archived documents.

The full results page lists up to ten cards and ten documents. Use **Next results** or **Previous results** to read more matches.
