---
title: "Profile"
description: "Where a user sees and changes their display name."
---

The *Profile* section of the account settings, at `/account/profile`, shows your
display name and your sign-in email. Change the display name and select **Save**.
To change the sign-in email, contact support.

## Live updates

The form does not change under your typing. When your display name changes in
another browser, a band appears above the form. Its link opens the current
profile in a new tab, and the fields of the open form keep what you typed.
Saving the form still works as before.
